Questions

What shop owners ask us

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We build your website and send you the link. If you don't like it, you owe nothing and we delete it.

I already have Instagram. Why a website?

Instagram is great for photos, but people searching on Google or Maps find websites first. A website also works for people without Instagram, and nobody can take it away from you.

Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. We do everything. When you want a change, send a message on WhatsApp the way you would to a friend.

Will it work on phones?

It's built for phones first, because that's where most of your customers will see it. It also looks right on computers and tablets.

How long does it take?

Your preview is ready in 48 hours. After you say yes, it's live on your own address within a day or two.

Is the website mine?

Yes. The address is registered in your name, and if you ever leave we hand you every file.
